# Working agreement

- Do not hand tasks back to the user that you can do yourself. That includes small ones such as reloading
  a page, taking a screenshot or checking a result. Do them, and report what you found.
- Only suggest that the user does something when it would take you hours and them minutes, or when it
  needs access you do not have. In that case say plainly what you cannot reach and why, as a limitation
  of your own check, and do not phrase it as an assignment for the user.
- If a check could not be run (for example no real GPU or no live market feed in the environment), state
  that once as a limit on what was verified. Do not end reports with "you need to look at this".
